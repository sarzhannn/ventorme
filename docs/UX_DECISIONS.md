# UX decisions — internal, do not deploy

| Table / observation | Hypothesis, not a fact of causality | Landing decision | How to verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1: 40.0% did not scroll | The value/action may be unclear above the fold | Problem choice and CTA on first phone viewport | First-click and 10-second comprehension test |
| T2: 80.5% phone visitors | Mobile friction is disproportionately important | 320px-first layout and bottom CTA | Device testing and task completion |
| T3–T4: Instagram mobile 10.6% conversion | Social visitors may need orientation; source is not proof of cause | Plain first-screen explanation of Ventorme and next step | Compare task success by acquisition context |
| T5: conversion varies by week | Context and traffic mix may vary | Treat time as a segment in future analysis | Compare comparable cohorts, not raw weeks |
| T6: phone non-scrollers converted 3.6% | Above-fold clarity is especially valuable | No prerequisite reading before action | Scroll-depth and CTA experiment |
| T7: short visits convert less often | Fast orientation may help | Short copy and a direct first choice | Timed usability test |
| T8: 175/184 finish; starters register more often | Completion is high among starters; self-selection may explain association | Short optional demo and quiz | Controlled experiment before causal claim |
| T9: cookie acceptance has 584 visitors | Raw clicks are not interest and labels cannot be ranked honestly | Benefit-specific but testable CTAs | A/B test with registration completion |
| T10: RU 78%, EN 20.9%, KZ 1.0% | KZ sample is very small | Complete RU/KZ/EN UI, no language inference | Test each locale with users |
| T11: 55.3% of registrants used Google | Existing Ventorme sign-in matters | External official registration only; no OAuth clone | Confirm external flow manually |
