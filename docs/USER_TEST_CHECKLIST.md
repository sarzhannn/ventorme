# Five-person user test — internal, do not deploy

Recruit at least five real school students. Do not coach them and do not record invented results.

1. Open the landing on a phone. Ask: “What is this site and what can you do here?” after 10 seconds.
2. Ask the participant to find a suitable tool for their current concern, first via the quiz and then without it.
3. Ask them to enter a free-text answer, go back, skip a question, change language, and restart.
4. Ask what they think happens after the registration button and whether the route feels like a guarantee.
5. Record only observed behavior: device, language, task success, hesitation points, quotes with consent, and proposed fix. Never invent interviews or conversion changes.
