# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, selected for a fast, dependency-free Hacknext delivery.

## Users

School students, especially mobile visitors arriving unfamiliar with Ventorme, who need a calm first step toward education and admission decisions.

## Product Purpose

Clarify a visitor's immediate question and direct them to the official Ventorme registration flow; it does not replace Ventorme or perform registration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three-question route finder, five deterministic routes, a pre-registration demo, and RU/KZ/EN. No backend, tracking, account creation, or storage of free-text answers. Every registration CTA uses team 113's supplied URL.

## Evidence on Hand

Official case data is held in `reference/` and must not be published. Claims are limited to supplied materials; user research is pending and must never be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the first useful action understandable before scrolling.
- Offer a route without forcing a quiz.
- State uncertainty and verification steps plainly.
- Preserve language choice while moving through the flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first semantic UI, keyboard access, visible focus, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support are required.
