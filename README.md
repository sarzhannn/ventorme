## What happens after school?

It's a simple question with surprisingly complicated answers.

Choosing a career. Finding a university. Preparing for exams. Understanding scholarships. Figuring out what to do first.

For many students, the problem isn't a lack of opportunities. It's knowing which opportunity actually matters to them.

**Ventorme already provides tools for educational planning. Our challenge was to make the first interaction with those tools more intuitive, engaging and personal.**

We built an interactive, student-centered landing page that turns uncertainty into a starting point.

> You don't need to have your entire future figured out. You just need to know where to begin.

## The challenge

**How might we turn students' initial interest into meaningful engagement and registrations on Ventorme?**

The HackNext 2026 case presented a clear conversion challenge.

| What the data showed | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **1,277** new visitors | Students were reaching the platform |
| **19.1%** registration conversion | Most visits did not result in registration |
| **40%** did not scroll | The first screen was a critical touchpoint |
| **80.5%** visited on mobile | The experience needed to prioritize smartphones |
| **14.4%** started the mini-quiz | Most visitors never entered an interactive discovery flow |
| **95.1%** of quiz starters finished | Students who began the quiz showed strong engagement |

*Source: analytics tables provided in the HackNext 2026 Ventorme case. These figures describe the original case data, not measured results of our new landing page.*

Our central insight:

**The challenge is not simply to show more features. It is to help students recognize which feature is relevant to their situation.**

## Our approach: Problem → Direction → Action

Instead of starting with a long catalog of tools, our landing page starts with a question:

**What's making it hard to decide?**

Students can identify the challenges they face and explore a more personalized path forward.

### 01. Start with a real concern

The opening screen focuses on familiar questions:

- What career should I choose?
- Which university is right for me?
- How can I find scholarships or grants?
- Which exams should I prepare for?

### 02. Answer a few quick questions

A short interactive questionnaire helps clarify the student's current situation, obstacles and desired outcome.

The experience is designed to be approachable rather than overwhelming.

### 03. Discover a direction

Based on the selected interests and answers, the landing page presents a relevant starting route.

Students can explore five areas:

| Direction | Focus |
|---|---|
| Career discovery | Understanding interests and potential career paths |
| University exploration | Finding and comparing educational options |
| Scholarships & funding | Learning where to begin researching financial support |
| Exam preparation | Planning around requirements such as IELTS and SAT |
| Admission planning | Turning a broad goal into manageable next steps |

### 04. Continue with Ventorme

Once students understand their next step, they can proceed to Ventorme's official platform.

Our landing page is the introduction, not a replacement for Ventorme.

## Designed around students, not just screens

We wanted the interface to feel closer to the audience it serves.

**A bold first impression.** Expressive typography, a vibrant purple-and-lime visual identity, interactive cards and purposeful motion make the opening experience more memorable.

**Mobile-first interaction.** Since smartphones account for the majority of visits in the case data, the interface emphasizes accessible controls, clear hierarchy and straightforward navigation.

**A personalized starting point.** The experience helps students move from a general concern to a more specific educational direction.

**Freedom to explore.** Students can browse available paths without being forced to complete the questionnaire first.

**Multilingual accessibility.** The landing page includes Russian, Kazakh and English language options.

**One official registration destination.** The project does not introduce a separate account system. Registration takes place through Ventorme.

## Research behind the experience

We approached the challenge as a product problem, not just a visual redesign.

### Quantitative analysis

We studied all 11 analytics tables supplied in the case, focusing on:

- Early exits and scrolling behavior
- Mobile versus desktop conversion
- Differences between acquisition channels
- Weekly fluctuations in registration rates
- Quiz engagement and completion
- Button interactions and language preferences
- Existing registration methods

One especially interesting observation was the relationship between quiz participation and registration.

Among mobile visitors in the case data, registration was substantially more common among those who started the quiz than among those who did not.

**This is an observed association, not proof that the quiz caused higher conversion.** It nevertheless gave us a useful hypothesis to explore through the new interface.

### Student-centered research

Alongside the analytics, we observed how students unfamiliar with Ventorme approached the original platform.

Our research focused on first impressions, finding relevant tools, understanding recommendations and identifying obstacles to registration.

These observations helped shape the project's emphasis on clearer navigation, accessible explanations and a recognizable first action.

### The hypothesis

> If students can identify their problem immediately and receive a relevant starting route, they may be more likely to explore Ventorme and proceed toward registration.

The landing page is our attempt to test that idea through design.

## The experience

**Interactive entry point**  
A problem-first hero that connects the student's uncertainty with a clear next action.

**Short discovery quiz**  
A guided flow that gathers the context needed to suggest a direction.

**Personalized routes**  
Educational pathways with practical steps and connections to relevant Ventorme capabilities.

**Exploration without commitment**  
An alternative way to browse opportunities for students who prefer to skip the quiz.

**Responsive visual system**  
A design built to work across screen sizes, with language and theme options.

**Official conversion flow**  
Calls to action leading to Ventorme's registration page using the team's HackNext tracking link.

---

## Project philosophy

We built this experience around three principles.

### Clarity before complexity

A student should understand what to do next without first understanding the entire platform.

### Relevance before registration

Show people why a tool matters to them before asking them to create an account.

### Guidance without pressure

Not every student is ready to make a final decision. A good first experience should help them explore, not make them feel behind.
